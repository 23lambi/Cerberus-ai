## Install with Cerberus Launcher

1. Download `cerberus-launcher-<your-platform>.zip` and extract it.
2. Open CerberusLauncher and choose **Install Cerberus**, then **Open Cerberus**.
3. Choose your AI engine in Settings → Setup check.

The launcher includes its Python runtime. Git, pip, and Python are not required
on the destination computer. Models and external AI engines are separate.

Available targets: Linux x64, Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon, and macOS Intel.
These initial builds are unsigned. Review the platform notes in
[the launcher guide](https://github.com/23lambi/Cerberus-ai/blob/main/docs/launcher.md)
before publishing this draft, including system requirements and signing status.
